ENGLISH LUDO GAME RULES

Board Flow: Both Team 1 and Team 2 start on their respective sides and progress towards tile 7 at the center. From 7, the path moves vertically upward through tiles 8, 9, 10, 11 to FINISH.

Challenges: Clicking a tile opens its challenge. Duplicate numbers (e.g. both 1s, both 2s) open the exact same challenge.

Card Mechanics: Each team gets 3 interactive cards used once per game:

  • Attack Card: Moves opposing team back 3 steps unless blocked by Defense.
  • Defense Card: Shields team against 1 incoming attack card.
  • Lucky Card: Protects team by skipping 1 challenge entirely.
